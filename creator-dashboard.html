Questions

How do I get credit for a sale?

When someone taps your link, your code is saved on their phone for 30 days. If they buy on that phone, you get the credit. They can also type your code at checkout. For courses paid by card, they need to type your code in the Payhip coupon box, so remind them in your posts.

When do I get paid?

Monthly, for the month before. Once you've earned at least BBD $20. Anything under that carries over to next month.

Why does a sale say Pending?

The order is in, but we're waiting for the payment, for example a card payment that hasn't cleared yet. It changes to Confirmed once the money arrives. Cancelled or refunded orders don't earn.

What do my followers get?

10% off courses, and from 10 Dec, 10% off their first Shop Bajan order. App prices aren't discounted.

Can I use my own code?

No. Orders you place yourself, or for someone else, don't earn commission.

Do I have to post a certain amount?

No. There's no minimum and no cost. Post when it fits your content.

How do I bring a small business on board?

WhatsApp us the business's name before they sign up, or ask them to mention your code. You get BBD $25 when they open their shop and make their first sale.
