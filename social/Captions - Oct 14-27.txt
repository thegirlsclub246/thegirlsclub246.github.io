Lash Tip Thursday 💗 How to make your clusters last 10–15 days:

1. Start with clean, oil-free lashes
2. Apply bond under your natural lashes, not on your skin
3. Clamp gently and seal before you go
4. Skip oil-based cleansers near your eyes
5. Brush them every morning

Save this for your next set, and shop trays at the link in our bio.

Lash Tip Thursday 💗 Aftercare do's and don'ts:

Do keep them dry for the first 24 hours
Do brush them gently each day
Don't sleep face-down in your pillow
Don't pull them off: use a lash remover
Don't use oil-based products near your eyes

Happy lashes last longer! Questions? Drop them below.
