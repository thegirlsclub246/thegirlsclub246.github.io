Good to know

Quick answers.

When does Shop Bajan open?

Thursday 10 December 2026. People on the waitlist hear first.

Does it cost anything to join the waitlist?

No. It’s free and there’s nothing to buy. We just need your name and a way to reach you.

What do I get for joining?

A waitlist-only launch gift (free delivery on your first order) and the first message when the doors open.

How do delivery and collection work?

At checkout you choose delivery, or collection or a meet-up. We handle both, so you’re not chasing sellers.

I run a small business. Can I sell here?

Yes. Handmade, imported, physical or digital, most categories are welcome. The one rule: We don’t sell food, liquor, wine or other alcohol. See the founding business details above, or message us and we’ll walk you through it.

What happens to my details?

We only use them to tell you about Shop Bajan. Our shop policies page has the full privacy details.
