Read the vendor agreement (18 points)
    Questions

    Good to know.

    Who sets my prices?

    You do. You set every price in BBD, and we never discount your products without your OK.

    Can I still sell on my own page and in my shop?

    Yes. Keep selling everywhere you do now. We only ask that you don't advertise the same product for less than its Marketplace price.

    How and when do I get paid?

    Every Sunday, for everything sold the week before, by bank transfer, FirstPay or BPWCCUL, with a statement of each sale.

    What if something doesn't sell?

    Each batch stays with us for 60 days. Then you can renew it, swap it for new stock, or collect it. Your products stay yours until they sell.

    What exactly does The Girls Club do for me?

    Think of us as your shop on the Bajan Amazon. You get your own shop page on our website with your logo, story and products. You drop off your stock with us in St. Thomas (or add a download link for digital products) and we keep it safe. When someone orders, we take the payment, pack the order, deliver it or arrange collection, and handle any questions or returns. Every Sunday we send you your share of the week before, and you can see every sale in your Vendor Portal. You set your own prices and keep selling everywhere you already do. On a $50 sale you keep $42.50 in your first year.

    What about returns and damaged items?

    We handle customer questions and returns. If an item is faulty because of the product, it comes back to you and the refund comes out of your next payout. If we damage something, we pay you for it.

    Is Starter really free?

    Yes. Starter has no monthly fee and needs no card. We take 15% of each sale in your first year (20% after that), which covers card fees, checkout, order handling and customer care. You keep 85% in your first year.

    Which plan should I pick?

    Starter is perfect to try us out. Every plan has the same per-sale fee, so Spotlight and Signature are for when you want your brand seen by more shoppers: homepage spots, shout-outs, posts and Reels.

    How does the free month work?

    Your first month on any paid plan is free. When you join, you set up your monthly plan payment by card through PayPal's secure page, but nothing is charged until your second month starts. Change or cancel your plan anytime before then.

    What is the per-sale fee for?

    It covers card fees, checkout, order handling and customer care, so you never have to deal with them. It's 15% of each sale for your first 12 months with us, then 20%. We'll remind you at least 30 days before it changes.

    Can I change my plan?

    Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or switch anytime. The new plan starts from your next monthly payment.

    What do I need to sign up?

    An email address and your phone. You create a free vendor login, add your brand details, plan and how you'd like to be paid, sign the agreement, then upload your own product photos and prices in your Vendor Portal. On a paid plan you'll also need a debit or credit card for PayPal (charged from month two).

    Can I update my products myself?

    Yes. In your Vendor Portal you can add products and photos, change prices and stock, and hide items anytime. New products and changes go live once Marishka approves them, normally within 2 working days. Stock changes update straight away.

    Is there a contract?

    Yes, a short consignment agreement you read and sign online when you create your vendor account. Either of us can end it with 14 days' notice.
