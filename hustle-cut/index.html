What it does

  • Pauses and ummm cut for you

    The quiet bits, the ummm and uhh, and repeated words are trimmed out, so your video moves fast. You can check every cut.

  • Captions that pop

    Words appear as you say them, in ten styles with animations, emoji and your own colour. Tap any word to fix it or cut it.

  • Zooms that keep eyes on you

    Gentle zoom-ins every few seconds, like the pros do.

  • Join a few clips

    Pick several clips and they're joined in order, with the gaps between them cut too.

  • Looks, effects and music

    One-tap looks, intros, colour filters, sound effects and free beats made for Hustle Cut.

  • Ready to post

    Save an MP4 straight to your phone in 9:16, 4:5, square or 16:9.

Yes. Open it in Safari or Chrome. Inside the Instagram, Facebook or TikTok app, Hustle Cut shows you how to open it in your browser, because saving may not work in there.

How long can my video be?

Up to 5 minutes in total. Short reels are fastest.

Will it catch every ummm?

It catches most of them, and you can tap any word to cut it yourself.
