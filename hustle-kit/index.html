Questions
I paid with PayPal but didn't set up my sign-in

Type the email you want to sign in with and the subscription ID from PayPal's email to you.

How do I sign in?

Paid with PayPal: after you pay, tell us the email you'll sign in with. Paid by card: sign in with the email you paid with. Then open Hustle Kit and we'll email you a sign-in link and a 6-digit code. No password needed.

Why is the price in US dollars?

PayPal and our card processor bill in US dollars. US$1 is BBD $2, so US$12 is about BBD $24. Your bank may add a small foreign exchange fee.

How do I cancel?

Paid with PayPal: log in to PayPal, go to Settings, then Payments, then Automatic payments, and cancel. Paid by card: use the link in your receipt email, or message us on Instagram @shopbajan and we'll cancel it. You keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

Can I put it on my home screen?

Yes. When you open Hustle Kit it shows you how, so it's one tap away like any other app.
